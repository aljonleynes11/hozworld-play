    Stealth

    Enemies lose you for 2 turns or until you attack. Usable every 3 turns.

    • Effects: status(hidden, self)

    Backstab

    Double damage while hidden. Breaks stealth.

    • Effects: bonus_if(hidden x2, caster) · damage(7-9)
    • Scales with: atk x0.55, lvl x0.35

    Shadowstep

    Slip to the tile behind an enemy within 4. Your next hit lands on its back.

    • Effects: warp(behind)

    Poison Blade

    Coat the blade: every hit you land for 3 turns also poisons.

    • Effects: status(poisonBlade, self)

    Smoke Bomb

    Drop smoke: enemies inside lose 2 range, and you are harder to hit.

    • Effects: zone(3x3, 2 turns, foes, start: status(smoked)) · status(smokeVeil, self)

    Steal

    A light cut that drains 1 AP next turn and may lift a trophy.

    • Effects: damage(2-3) · steal(ap 1)
    • Scales with: atk x0.24, lvl x0.35

    Assassinate

    Triple damage to a target below 25% HP.

    • Effects: lethal(below 25%, x3) · damage(8-11)
    • Scales with: atk x0.74, lvl x0.35

    Vanish Strike

    A dagger strike that doubles its damage when you are hidden.

    • Effects: bonus_if(hidden x2, caster) · damage(4-5)
    • Scales with: atk x0.42, lvl x0.35

    Slip Away

    Slips into the smoke: 50% less damage taken for 1 turn.

    • Effects: status(guarded, self)

    Toxic Cut

    A dagger cut laced with poison: 2 damage a turn for 2 turns.

    • Effects: damage(3-4) · status(poison 2x2)
    • Scales with: atk x0.3, lvl x0.35

    Ambush Lunge

    Lunges at a foe 2 tiles away in a straight line, dashing in. Doubles its damage when you are hidden.

    • Effects: when(dist 2 & in_line: gap_close(2)) · bonus_if(hidden x2, caster) · damage(5-6)
    • Scales with: atk x0.46, lvl x0.35

    Blade Oil

    Oils the blade for 5 turns: every hit you land poisons the target.

    • Effects: status(poisonBlade, self)

    Cloak Mark

    For 2 turns, every hit from behind marks the foe: it takes 25% more damage.

    • Effects: aura(on backstab, 2 turns, then status(marked))

    Venom Slash

    A poisoned arc at the tile you aim at, hitting it and the two tiles beside it. Every foe hit is poisoned.

    • Effects: area(front3)
    • Scales with: atk x0.36, lvl x0.35

    Light Feet

    Light on your feet: you start your next turn with 1 more MP.

    • Effects: status(mpBoost, self)

    Knife Rain

    Blades fly out in every direction: every adjacent foe takes damage.

    • Effects: around(1)
    • Scales with: atk x0.4, lvl x0.35
