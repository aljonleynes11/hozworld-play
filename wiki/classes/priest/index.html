    Judgment

    A searing bolt with the force of your arm behind it. It also shields you a little.

    • Effects: damage(6-8) · shield_bonus(6%)
    • Scales with: int x0.7, lvl x0.91

    Mending Tide

    A cool tide that mends an ally, or crashes on a foe.

    • Effects: when(allies: heal_magic(13%)) · when(foes: damage(16-18))
    • Scales with: luk x0.35, lvl x0.63

    Divine Pull

    A gust hauls a unit 2 tiles toward you (allies too; +10 on collision) and buffets it for a little damage.

    • Effects: damage(5-7) · push(2, collide 10, pull)
    • Scales with: agi x1.01, lvl x0.35

    Purify

    Washes away every debuff on an ally (or you) and heals a little.

    • Effects: cleanse · heal_magic(7%)
    • Scales with: lvl x0.5

    Radiance

    Fire bursts where it lands: every unit in the 3×3 takes fire damage.

    • Effects: blast(square 1)
    • Scales with: int x1.0, lvl x1.1

    Sanctuary

    Consecrates a 3×3 patch for 3 turns, on a tile up to 3 away or on your own tile: allies on it heal each turn, foes take water damage.

    • Effects: zone(3x3, 3 turns, start: when(allies: heal_magic(6%)) · when(foes: damage(2-3)))
    • Scales with: luk x0.1625, lvl x0.43

    Radiant Ray

    A rushing gust down a line of 5 tiles from you toward the aimed tile. Foes in it are pushed 1 tile back (+8 on collision).

    • Effects: area(line(5))
    • Scales with: agi x0.94, lvl x0.3

    Bless

    Bless an ally (or yourself): +20% damage for 2 turns.

    • Effects: status(blessed)

    Shield Charge

    Charge up to 2 tiles into a foe: the blow lands, and a searing ward shields you.

    • Effects: when(dist 2+: gap_close(2)) · damage(5-7) · shield_bonus(10%) · range cannot be modified
    • Scales with: int x0.165, lvl x0.432

    Grace Step

    A ripple carries you to any empty tile within 3, healing yourself and every ally next to the tile you land on.

    • Effects: warp(tile) · heal_magic(5%) · around(1) · range cannot be modified
    • Scales with: luk x0.125, lvl x0.35

    Swap

    Trade places with any unit within 4 tiles.

    • Effects: swap · range cannot be modified

    Holy Shield

    Absorbs damage for 2 turns or until used up. Usable on any ally or you.

    • Effects: shield(18%)
    • Scales with: lvl x0.5

    Aegis

    Raises the aegis: you and every ally within 2 gain a shield, and every foe within 2 takes fire damage.

    • Effects: around(2) · shield_bonus(12%) · around(2)
    • Scales with: int x1.4, lvl x1.47

    Miracle

    A miracle: you and every ally within 3 are healed, and every foe within 3 takes water damage.

    • Effects: heal_magic(14%) · around(3) · around(3)
    • Scales with: luk x0.725, lvl x0.93

    Rapture

    A gust seizes every unit within 3 tiles of the target (allies too) and drags it in; each one that hits something takes collision damage.

    • Effects: pull_in(r3, collide 12) · damage(4-6)
    • Scales with: agi x2.03, lvl x0.35

    Divine Grace

    The first killing blow this fight leaves you at 1 HP, and nothing can kill you until your next turn starts. Once per fight.

    • Effects: status(divineGrace, self)
