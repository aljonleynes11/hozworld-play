    Priest

    Main stat: INT.

    Basic attack: Smite, 2-3 damage, Holy.

    Heal

    Heals a living ally. Against undead it deals Holy damage instead.

    • Effects: heal_magic(13%)
    • Scales with: matk x0.88, lvl x0.35

    Holy Shield

    Absorbs damage for 2 turns or until used up.

    • Effects: shield(18%)
    • Scales with: matk x0.98, lvl x0.35

    Bless

    Bless an ally (or yourself): +20% damage for 2 turns.

    • Effects: status(blessed)

    Swap

    Trade places with any unit within 4 tiles.

    • Effects: swap

    Purify

    Washes away every debuff on an ally (or you) and heals a little.

    • Effects: cleanse · heal_magic(7%)
    • Scales with: matk x0.45, lvl x0.35

    Sanctuary

    Consecrates the 3×3 ground around you for 3 turns: allies heal a turn, undead burn.

    • Effects: zone(3x3, 3 turns, start: heal_magic(6%))
    • Scales with: matk x0.33, lvl x0.35

    Divine Grace

    The first killing blow this fight leaves you at 1 HP instead. Once per fight.

    • Effects: status(divineGrace, self)

    Renew

    Renews an ally: it heals a little at the start of each of its turns for 3 turns.

    • Effects: status(renewed)
    • Scales with: matk x0.3, lvl x0.35

    Repel

    A flash of light: damage, and a foe in a straight line is pushed 1 tile back (+6 on collision).

    • Effects: damage(2-3) · push(1, collide 6, in_line)
    • Scales with: matk x0.36, lvl x0.35

    Judgment

    A holy bolt. Undead take much more of it (Holy vs undead).

    • Effects: damage(5-6)
    • Scales with: matk x0.48, lvl x0.35

    Mending

    A holy ring on a plus around the target: heals each ally in it and gives them a shield.

    • Effects: blast(plus 1, allies)
    • Scales with: matk x0.4, lvl x0.35

    Radiant Ray

    A beam of light down a line of 5 tiles from you toward the aimed tile.

    • Effects: area(line(5))
    • Scales with: matk x0.5, lvl x0.35

    Protection

    Lays a warding seal on an ally: it takes 20% less damage for 2 turns.

    • Effects: status(guarded)

    Radiant Vow

    For 3 turns, each holy hit you land renews you: you heal a little at the start of your next 2 turns.

    • Effects: aura(holy, any hit, 3 turns, then status(renewed, self))

    Hallowed Ground

    Consecrates the 3x3 ground you aim at for 3 turns: undead on it take holy damage each turn.

    • Effects: zone(3x3, 3 turns, foes, start: when(undead: heal_magic(4%)))
    • Scales with: matk x0.3, lvl x0.3

    Divine Pull

    A beam of light hauls a foe 2 tiles toward you (+8 on collision). It also takes a little holy damage.

    • Effects: damage(3-4) · push(2, collide 8, pull)
    • Scales with: matk x0.42, lvl x0.35
