    Swordsman

    Main stat: POW.

    Basic attack: Sword, 3-5 damage, Neutral.

    Charge

    Strikes a target up to 2 tiles away. In a straight line it dashes in, shoves the target 1 tile back and follows; +8 if it collides. Off-line, it only strikes.

    • Effects: when(dist 2 & in_line: gap_close(1)) · damage(4-5) · push(1, collide 8, follow, in_line)
    • Scales with: atk x0.36, lvl x0.35

    Bash

    30% to stun: the target loses its next turn.

    • Effects: damage(5-6) · status(stunned, 30%)
    • Scales with: atk x0.48, lvl x0.35

    Guard

    Braces behind the shield: 30% less damage for 2 turns (+5% a rank).

    • Effects: status(guarded, self)

    Cleave

    A wide swing at any adjacent tile: hits it and the two tiles beside it, even with no one there.

    • Effects: area(front3)
    • Scales with: atk x0.53, lvl x0.35

    Taunt

    Every enemy within 2 tiles must attack you on its next turn.

    • Effects: nearby(r2: taunted)

    Leap Strike

    Leap exactly 2 tiles to a free tile and hit every enemy next to the landing.

    • Effects: warp(tile) · around(1)
    • Scales with: atk x0.48, lvl x0.35

    Whirlwind

    Spin: every adjacent enemy takes damage and is pushed 1 tile back.

    • Effects: around(1)
    • Scales with: atk x0.54, lvl x0.35

    Ram

    Hits a foe 2-3 tiles away. In a straight line it dashes in, then shoves the foe 2 tiles back and follows; +10 if it collides. Off-line, it only strikes.

    • Effects: when(dist 2+ & in_line: gap_close(2)) · damage(3-4) · push(2, collide 10, follow, in_line)
    • Scales with: atk x0.45, lvl x0.35

    Shield Wall

    Locks the shield up: 40% less damage taken for 3 turns.

    • Effects: status(guarded, self)

    Concussion

    A hard blow: 35% to stun the target, so it loses its next turn. Bosses shrug off the stun.

    • Effects: damage(5-6) · status(stunned, 35%)
    • Scales with: atk x0.44, lvl x0.35

    Battle Cry

    A war cry: +20% damage for 3 turns.

    • Effects: status(blessed, self)

    Quake

    Stamps the ground: every adjacent enemy takes damage and is pushed 1 tile back, +8 on collision.

    • Effects: around(1)
    • Scales with: atk x0.48, lvl x0.35

    Armor Break

    A cracking blow that leaves the foe open: for 2 turns it takes 25% more damage from every source. Works on bosses.

    • Effects: damage(4-5) · status(marked)
    • Scales with: atk x0.4, lvl x0.35

    Bulwark

    A wide swing across the three to five tiles ahead. Each foe it hits takes damage and is taunted for 2 turns.

    • Effects: area(front5)
    • Scales with: atk x0.42, lvl x0.35

    Crash Aura

    For 3 turns, every time you push a foe, it takes a crash of extra damage on top of the shove.

    • Effects: aura(on push, 3 turns, then damage(3-4))

    Shatter

    Breaks a stunned foe: double damage on it, then it is knocked 1 tile back, +8 if it hits a wall.

    • Effects: bonus_if(stunned x2) · damage(6-8) · push(1, collide 8)
    • Scales with: atk x0.5, lvl x0.35
