    Basic attack: Sword, 3-5 damage, Neutral.

    Bash

    30% to stun: the target loses its next turn.

    • Effects: damage(5-6) · status(stunned, 30%)
    • Scales with: str x1.375, lvl x1.45

    Blood Strike

    A cold cut that feeds you: you heal 30% of the damage it deals.

    • Effects: damage(5-7, heals 30%)
    • Scales with: luk x1.07, lvl x1.45

    Searing Lunge

    A searing lunge that reaches 2 tiles and sets the target burning for 2 turns.

    • Effects: damage(5-6) · status(burn 3x2)
    • Scales with: int x1.375, lvl x1.45, dot_int x0.2, dot_lvl x0.16

    Guard

    Braces behind the shield: 30% less damage for 2 turns.

    • Effects: status(guarded, self)

    Cleave

    A wide swing at any adjacent tile: hits it and the two tiles beside it, even with no one there.

    • Effects: area(front3)
    • Scales with: str x1.525, lvl x1.57

    Quake Jump

    Leap onto a free tile 2-3 away, or stomp where you stand: every unit next to you takes damage and is pushed 1 tile back, +8 on collision.

    • Effects: jump(2-3 or stay) · around(1) · range cannot be modified
    • Scales with: luk x1.21, lvl x1.55

    Flame Arc

    Sweeps a cone of fire toward the tile you aim at, 1-2 tiles away: 1 tile at range 1, 3 at range 2. Everything caught burns for 2 turns.

    • Effects: area(cone(2))
    • Scales with: int x1.5, lvl x1.55, dot_int x0.25, dot_lvl x0.2

    Bulwark

    A wide swing at a tile beside you: every unit in the 5-tile front arc takes damage and is taunted for 2 turns.

    • Effects: area(front5)
    • Scales with: lvl x3.4

    Charge

    Strikes a target up to 2 tiles away. In a straight line it dashes in, shoves the target 1 tile back and follows; +8 if it collides. Off-line, it only strikes.

    • Effects: when(dist 2 & in_line: gap_close(1)) · damage(4-5) · push(1, collide 8, follow, in_line) · range cannot be modified
    • Scales with: str x1.025, lvl x1.17

    Iron Pull

    Hauls a unit 2-3 tiles away 2 tiles toward you (allies too; +8 on collision) and cuts it on the way in, pinning it for 1 turn.

    • Effects: damage(3-4) · push(2, collide 8, pull) · status(rooted) · range cannot be modified
    • Scales with: luk x0.79, lvl x1.15

    Flame Leap

    Leap 1-3 tiles to a free tile; the flames on landing hit every unit next to it.

    • Effects: warp(tile) · around(1) · range cannot be modified
    • Scales with: int x1.0, lvl x1.15

    Battle Cry

    A war cry: +20% damage for 3 turns.

    • Effects: status(blessed, self)

    Shatter

    Breaks a stunned unit: double damage on it, then it is knocked 1 tile back, +8 if it hits a wall.

    • Effects: bonus_if(stunned x2) · damage(6-8) · push(1, collide 8)
    • Scales with: str x2.75, lvl x2.55

    Rage Slam

    The LUK build's ultimate: one heavy slam on an adjacent foe.

    • Effects: damage(8-11)
    • Scales with: luk x2.14, lvl x2.55

    Rune Burst

    Hurls a rune at a foe 1-3 tiles away: the 3×3 around it takes the hit. A foe that is burning or chilled loses the brand, takes double, and the rune lands with the brand's element.

    • Effects: blast(square 1)
    • Scales with: int x2.75, lvl x2.55

    Armor Break

    A cracking blow that leaves the foe open: for 2 turns it takes 25% more damage from every source. Works on bosses.

    • Effects: damage(4-5) · status(marked)
    • Scales with: lvl x3.3
