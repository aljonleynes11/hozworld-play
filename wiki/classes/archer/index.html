    Archer

    Main stat: AGI.

    Basic attack: Bow shot, 4-5 damage, Neutral.

    Push Arrow

    Knocks the target back 1 tile if it is in a straight line. +8 if it hits a wall, tree or unit.

    • Effects: damage(3-4) · push(1, collide 8, in_line)
    • Scales with: atk x0.54, lvl x0.35

    Poison Arrow

    Weak hit, but poisons for 2 turns with a scaled tick.

    • Effects: damage(2-3) · status(poison 3x2)
    • Scales with: atk x0.28, lvl x0.35, dot_atk x0.16, dot_lvl x0.1

    Retreat Shot

    A drawn-out shot, then you hop 1 tile back from the target. Costs no MP.

    • Effects: damage(2-3) · back_off(1)
    • Scales with: atk x0.24, lvl x0.35

    Volley

    Three arrows fan out across the aimed area.

    • Effects: blast(square 1)
    • Scales with: atk x0.32, lvl x0.35

    Pinning Shot

    Pins the target: it cannot move on its next turn.

    • Effects: damage(3-4) · status(rooted)
    • Scales with: atk x0.39, lvl x0.35

    Hawk Eye

    Sharpens your aim for 2 turns: +2 range and +20% crit chance.

    • Effects: status(hawkEye, self)

    Arrow Rain

    Arrows fall on the aimed tile and the 4 beside it for 2 turns: anything ending its turn inside is hit.

    • Effects: zone(plus1, 2 turns, start: damage(5-7))
    • Scales with: atk x0.45, lvl x0.35

    Backstep Shot

    Shoots, then hops 2 tiles back out of reach.

    • Effects: damage(2-3) · back_off(2)
    • Scales with: atk x0.3, lvl x0.35

    Gale Shot

    A gust-borne arrow. A foe in a straight line is pushed 2 tiles back; +8 if it collides.

    • Effects: damage(3-4) · push(2, collide 8, in_line)
    • Scales with: atk x0.48, lvl x0.35

    Spread

    Poison-tipped arrows burst on the aimed tile: every foe in the 3×3 is hit and poisoned.

    • Effects: blast(square 1)
    • Scales with: atk x0.26, lvl x0.35, dot_atk x0.14

    Windrunner

    A gust at your back: +1 MP at the start of your next turn.

    • Effects: status(mpBoost, self)

    Venomcraft

    For 2 turns, each hit on a poisoned foe makes its poison last 1 turn longer.

    • Effects: aura(hit on poison, 2 turns, then extend_poison(+1))

    Keen Eye

    Your eye settles for 2 turns: your hits deal 20% more damage.

    • Effects: status(blessed, self)

    Snapback

    Shoots, then springs 1 tile back. A foe in a straight line is pushed 1 tile; +8 if it collides.

    • Effects: damage(3-4) · push(1, collide 8, in_line) · back_off(1)
    • Scales with: atk x0.42, lvl x0.35

    Plague Cloud

    A poisoned arrow bursts into a cloud over the 3×3 for 2 turns. Foes in it start their turn poisoned.

    • Effects: zone(3x3, 2 turns, foes, start: status(poison 2x1))
    • Scales with: atk x0.24, lvl x0.35, dot_atk x0.14

    Barb Trap

    Sets a barbed glyph on the aimed tile for 4 turns. The first foe to step onto it is poisoned for 2 turns.

    • Effects: zone(1x1, 4 turns, trigger enter, foes, start: status(poison 2x2))
    • Scales with: atk x0.22, lvl x0.3, dot_atk x0.12
