    Mage

    Main stat: INT.

    Basic attack: Staff bolt, 3-5 damage, Neutral.

    Frost Bolt

    30% to freeze: the target loses its next turn.

    • Effects: damage(5-7) · status(frozen, 30%)
    • Scales with: matk x0.44, lvl x0.35

    Lightning

    Double damage to a frozen target.

    • Effects: bonus_if(frozen x2) · damage(6-8)
    • Scales with: matk x0.55, lvl x0.35

    Fireball

    Bursts where it lands: every foe in the 3×3 takes the hit and burns.

    • Effects: blast(square 1)
    • Scales with: matk x0.44, lvl x0.35, dot_matk x0.12, dot_lvl x0.08

    Ice Wall

    Raises a 3-tile ice wall for 2 turns. It blocks movement and line of sight.

    • Effects: zone(wall, 2 turns, blocks)

    Blink

    Teleport to any empty tile you can see within 3.

    • Effects: warp(tile)

    Chain Lightning

    Hits the target, then bounces to up to 2 more foes within 2 tiles at −25% a jump.

    • Effects: chain(2, x0.75)
    • Scales with: matk x0.45, lvl x0.35

    Meteor

    Marks a 3×3 area. The meteor lands at the start of your next turn.

    • Effects: zone(3x3, 3 turns, delay 1, end: damage(13-17))
    • Scales with: matk x0.97, lvl x0.35

    Frost Nova

    A burst of cold around you: every adjacent foe takes damage, and 25% of them freeze.

    • Effects: around(1)
    • Scales with: matk x0.4, lvl x0.35

    Flame Dart

    A quick fire dart. The target burns for 1 turn.

    • Effects: damage(3-4) · status(burn 3x1)
    • Scales with: matk x0.36, lvl x0.35, dot_matk x0.1

    Arc Focus

    Gathers arc sparks: +1 MP at the start of your next turn.

    • Effects: status(mpBoost, self)

    Ember Trail

    Lays a plus of embers for 2 turns. Foes standing in it at the start of their turn burn.

    • Effects: zone(plus1, 2 turns, start: status(burn 2x1))
    • Scales with: matk x0.25, lvl x0.3

    Ice Lance

    A heavy ice shard: damage, and the target is snared in place.

    • Effects: damage(6-7) · status(snared)
    • Scales with: matk x0.48, lvl x0.35

    Inferno

    A ball of fire bursts where it lands: every foe in the 3×3 takes the hit and burns for 2 turns.

    • Effects: blast(square 1)
    • Scales with: matk x0.5, lvl x0.35, dot_matk x0.12

    Frostbite

    Frost coats you for 2 turns: each ice hit you land also snares the foe in place. Bosses are immune.

    • Effects: aura(water, any hit, 2 turns, then status(snared))

    Burning Brand

    Your fire hits brand the foe for 2 turns: a marked foe takes 25% more damage from every source.

    • Effects: aura(fire, any hit, 2 turns, then status(marked))

    Overcharge

    Stores arc energy for 2 turns: each wind hit you land charges your next hit for 1.5 times the damage.

    • Effects: aura(wind, any hit, 2 turns, then status(charged, self))
