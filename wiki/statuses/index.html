    Statuses

    Every status a unit can carry. Ailments hurt or hold a unit back; Buffs help the unit that carries them. Bosses shrug off the crowd control marked below. Numbers are the defaults: the skills on each class or monster page give the values they use.

    Ailments

    Frozen

    frozen

    • Does: Loses its next turn, then the ice shatters. Bosses are immune.
    • Applied by: Frost Bolt

    Stunned

    stunned

    • Does: Loses its next turn. Bosses are immune.
    • Applied by: Bash, Concussion, Shield Bash (Orc Guard Captain), Shield Bash (Bone Knight)

    Poisoned

    poison

    • Does: Takes damage at the start of each of its turns, for a set number of turns. Undead are immune.
    • Element: Poison
    • Applied by: Poison Arrow, Toxic Cut, Plague Cloud, Barb Trap, Pollen Puff (Pollen Sprout), Stab (Orc Youngling), Venom Bite (Beetle), Rusty Blade (Bone Knight), Gnaw (Rat), Sting (Bee), Venom Fang (Snake)

    Burning

    burn

    • Does: Takes fire damage at the start of each of its turns, for a set number of turns.
    • Element: Fire
    • Applied by: Flame Dart, Ember Trail

    Sticky

    sticky

    • Does: Loses 1 MP at the start of its next turn. MP resist reduces the loss. Bosses are immune.
    • Applied by: Slam (Slime)

    Snared

    snared

    • Does: Loses 1 MP at the start of its next turn. MP resist reduces the loss. Bosses are immune.
    • Applied by: Ice Lance, Frostbite, Goo Snare (Small Slime)

    Underground

    burrowed

    • Does: Underground: it can't be targeted and loses its next turn. It pops up beside the hero.
    • Applied by: Burrow (Small Worm)

    Rattled

    rattled

    • Does: Loses AP at the start of its next turn. The skill sets how much. Bosses are immune.
    • Applied by: Sap Spit (Thorn Sprout), Cry (Orc Youngling), War Cry (Orc Warchief), Rattle (Skeleton), Dread Roar (Bone Knight)

    Robbed

    robbed

    • Does: Loses 1 AP at the start of its next turn.
    • Applied by: Steal

    Taunted

    taunted

    • Does: Must attack the unit that taunted it, while that unit is in reach. 2 rounds. Bosses are immune.
    • Applied by: Taunt

    Pinned

    rooted

    • Does: Can't walk on its next turn, but can still act. 1 round. Bosses are immune.
    • Applied by: Pinning Shot, Vine Lash (Man-eater Flower), Bramble Bite (Bramble Sprout), Pinning Shot (Orc Archer)

    Smoked

    smoked

    • Does: Its range is cut by 2 tiles while it stands in smoke. 2 rounds.
    • Applied by: Smoke Bomb

    Marked

    marked

    • Does: Takes 25% more damage from every source. 2 rounds.
    • Applied by: Armor Break, Burning Brand, Cloak Mark

    Buffs

    Hidden

    hidden

    • Does: Enemies can't see it. Ends after 2 rounds, or when it attacks.
    • Applied by: Stealth

    Boosted

    mpBoost

    • Does: Gains 1 MP at the start of its next turn.
    • Applied by: Windrunner, Arc Focus, Light Feet, Ooze Boost (Small Slime)

    Reflect

    reflect

    • Does: Sends back half of each hit it takes, up to its max HP. 2 rounds.
    • Applied by: Reflect (Beetle)

    Shielded

    shielded

    • Does: Absorbs damage from its shield pool. Ends after 2 rounds or when the pool is empty.
    • Applied by: Holy Shield

    Coiled

    coiled

    • Does: +25% DEF for 2 rounds.
    • Applied by: Coil (Snake)

    Guarded

    guarded

    • Does: Takes 30% less damage, +5 points a rank. 2 rounds.
    • Applied by: Guard, Shield Wall, Slip Away, Protection, Guard Up (Orc Warrior), Rally (Orc Guard Captain)

    Hawk Eye

    hawkEye

    • Does: +2 range and +20% crit chance. 2 rounds.
    • Applied by: Hawk Eye

    Poisoned Blade

    poisonBlade

    • Does: Its hits poison the target, if the target is not poisoned yet. 3 rounds.
    • Applied by: Poison Blade, Blade Oil

    Blessed

    blessed

    • Does: Deals 20% more damage. 2 rounds.
    • Applied by: Bless, Battle Cry, Keen Eye, War Chant (Orc Shaman), Blood Rage (Orc Berserker), Enrage (Orc Warchief)

    Smoke Veil

    smokeVeil

    • Does: +40 dodge while it stands in its own smoke. 2 rounds.
    • Applied by: Smoke Bomb

    Divine Grace

    divineGrace

    • Does: The first killing blow this fight leaves it at 1 HP.
    • Applied by: Divine Grace

    Renewed

    renewed

    • Does: Heals at the start of each of its turns, by a share of the caster's magic. 3 rounds.
    • Applied by: Renew, Radiant Vow

    Charged

    charged

    • Does: Its next damage hit deals 1.5x. Lasts 3 rounds if unused.
    • Applied by: Overcharge

    Aura

    elementAura

    • Does: After each of its skill hits, an extra effect runs on the target, for a set number of rounds.
    • Applied by: Venomcraft, Crash Aura, Frostbite, Burning Brand, Overcharge, Cloak Mark, Radiant Vow
