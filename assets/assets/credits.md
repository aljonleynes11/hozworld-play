## Orcs (`assets/sprites/orcs/`)

Composed from Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator layers (Liberated Pixel Cup). The skin is recoloured green in the sheet, cloth, leather and iron are recoloured into tribal ramps (brown, dark red, bone, rust, iron, black), and the war-paint head tint is applied to `orc_berserker`. Walk sheets are `orc_<name>.png`, with `_idle`, `_hurt` and one attack sheet each (`_slash_body`, `_thrust`, `_shoot` or `_spellcast`).
